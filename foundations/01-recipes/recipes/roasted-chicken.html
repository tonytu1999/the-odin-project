Roasted Chicken

Description

This is a recipe of making roasted chicken.

Ingredients

  • 4 boneless chicken breast halves with skin
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder

Steps

  1. Season chicken on both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat olive oil whith medium heat. Place chicken breast skin-side down
  3. Cook for 5-7 minutes until skin is golden brown and crispy.
  4. Flip chicken and cook for another 5-7 minutes until cooked through.
  5. Remove chicken from the pan and let it rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
